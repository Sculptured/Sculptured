---
title: Control live agents
description: Message an agent, interrupt its current turn, request a summary or stop its session.
---

Click an agent on the board, or its name on the **Agents** tab, to open its page. Read and message it on its **Conversation** tab, and interrupt or stop it on its **Activity** tab. **Start team** and **Stop team…** in the project's tab row apply to the whole team.

## The agent's page

| Part | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Header** | **Board** (<kbd>Esc</kbd>) goes back to the board as you left it. Then the agent's name, its state, and what it runs on (Claude or Codex, and the model). |
| **Conversation** | The transcript (your messages, the agent's replies, its tool calls and their results), then the message box. <kbd>⏎</kbd> sends, <kbd>⇧⏎</kbd> adds a line. The search icon in the tab row (or <kbd>/</kbd>) searches the transcript. |
| **Setup** | The agent's design: name, what it runs on, Job, folder, connections, and Advanced. See [Compose on the board](/guides/board). |
| **Activity** | **Interrupt**, **Soft stop** and **Hard stop…**, then its check, folder, session number, tokens in and out, and the evidence for its latest status. Below them, **Progress** shows tool calls, files changed, commands and checks as a picture (see [Context and communication](/guides/context)), and **Raw events** shows every recorded event verbatim, the evidence behind everything else. |

Conversation is the first tab once the team has started. Before that, the page opens on Setup.

Notices appear above the transcript when they apply: a question waiting for you, "isn't signed in on the computer", a stall warning, whom it is waiting for, and the reason for its last status change. **Hand over again** is on the Activity tab, and also above the transcript when the agent's CLI was signed out.

## Send guidance

Type in the message box and press <kbd>⏎</kbd>. The **⋯** next to it sets the message's intent:

| Intent | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Inform** (default) | Waits for the current turn to end. The agent reads it on its next turn. |
| **Instruct** | May interrupt. With **Interrupt current turn** ticked, the current turn is cut off and the agent reads your message next. |

Each message shows whether it was queued, delivered or read. **Delivered** means the agent's computer accepted the message. **Read** means a turn read it. Neither means the agent did what you asked.

Messages can only go to a running agent. Otherwise the box says why, for example "Status: stopped; messages can only be sent to a running agent."

<Tip>The first agent of a team without a Job shows "Your next message is this agent's work and starts the team's handoffs." Whatever you send it is the team's work.</Tip>

## Interrupt, wrap up or stop

Open the agent's **Activity** tab. The controls are at the top.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Interrupt** (<kbd>i</kbd>, on any of the agent's tabs) | Cuts off the current turn so you can redirect the agent. It is off when no turn is in progress, or when this kind of agent can't be interrupted. |
| **Soft stop · ~5K** | Asks the agent to finish the current task, summarize and hand off, then stop. Sculptured stops the session after the wrap-up turn, about 5,000 more output tokens, or 5 minutes, whichever comes first. The button reads **Wrapping up…** meanwhile. |
| **Hard stop…** | Stops the agent's process after you confirm, with no wrap-up turn. Agents it started stop too. "Messages it has not read yet will be marked as not delivered." |
| **Stop team…** (the project's tab row) | Stops every running agent in the team. "Their history is kept." |

<Warning>Usage reports can arrive late, so a soft stop can go past 5,000 output tokens. The wrap-up message tells the agent never to claim success to finish faster. Still read its summary as agent-reported.</Warning>

After a soft stop, Activity says "Wrap-up finished; session stopped." When an output budget stopped it, Activity says "Output budget reached; session stopped."

## Stalled agents

Its Conversation tab may show **Stalled (heuristic)** above the transcript, with the reason, when an agent:

- keeps hitting the same error, three times in a row; or
- has had no output for 30 minutes in the middle of its turn, for example a command that never finishes. An agent waiting for your answer to a question is not counted.

This is an inference from recent events, labelled as a heuristic, and the agent also shows under **Needs you**. Sculptured does not intervene automatically. You can:

- **Nudge**: interrupt the agent with a message. One that keeps failing is asked to summarize what it tried and try a different approach; one that went quiet is asked to stop anything that will not finish, say where it is, and continue;
- **Soft stop**: ask it to wrap up.

A silent agent's flag clears as soon as it produces output again.

## Read the state accurately

- **An open, idle session is not working.** "No active work; sessions are open" means nothing is in progress.
- **Unknown is not stopped.** While an agent's computer is unreachable, the agent is *stale*: its activity is the last known one, and it may still be running. See [When the computer drops off](/guides/offline).
- **Run shows the running agents.** The **Agents** tab lists every agent, including stopped and planned ones. The Design board keeps the full design.
- **Each status has evidence.** On the Activity tab, "status" names who said so, such as observed by the computer or inferred by Sculptured.

The full table is in [Agent states](/reference/states).

## Agents that start agents

Agents can create helpers with `spawn_agent`, message each other with `send_agent_message`, and read the team with `team_status`. These helpers are **managed children**:

- they inherit their parent's folder, model and permissions;
- they run on the same computer as their parent;
- they appear on the Run board with their parent. **Focus selected subtree** shows one branch, **Up to parent** widens it, and **Whole tree** goes back.

There are **no limits on children unless you set them**. To set them, open the team's **Settings** tab and then **More settings** (with the team stopped):

| Setting | What it limits | When it is reached |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Most agents, children included** | Every agent in the team at once: the ones you designed plus every child. | The agent is told "Team limit you set: 20 agents, children included" and carries on without a new child. |
| **Most levels of children** | How deep children may go. 1 lets your agents start children that cannot start their own. | The agent is told "Team limit you set: 3 levels of children". |

Leave a field blank for no limit. When a limit stops a spawn, the parent's card on the Run board says so.

<Frame caption="What to look for: **Limits on spawned agents** under **More settings** in the team's Settings. Blank means no limit.">
  ![The team's Settings page with More settings open: Limits on spawned agents, with Most agents, children included and Most levels of children both set to no limit](/images/guides/team-limits.png)
</Frame>

The Run board stays readable with many children. A parent with more than six shows five of them and a **+N more** card that lists the rest. Children below the third level fold into a **+N deeper** card. Either card opens a list of those agents with their status, and each opens that agent. The **Agents** tab always lists every agent. A token limit or the team's output budget still applies to children, so set one of those to cap cost.

<Frame caption="What to look for: the **+N more** card next to the drawn children. It opens a list of the rest.">
  ![The Run board: a Lead researcher card with arrows to five of its helper agents, and a +3 more card for the helpers that are not drawn](/images/guides/more-children.png)
</Frame>

A hard stop of a parent stops its descendants first. Helpers that a CLI starts by itself, and other processes an agent launches, are not managed children. Sculptured does not track or stop them.

## Full screen

**Expand editor** shows on the message box when you point at it or type in it (always on a touch screen). It opens the conversation full screen: the agent's name, the transcript search, the transcript and the message box fill the window, for long reading or writing. What you typed, and where you were in the transcript, stay as they were. **Exit full screen** (or <kbd>Esc</kbd>) returns to the agent's page.

The Job box on an agent's Setup tab has its own **Expand editor**, which opens the Job in a full-window editor; **Done** returns to the page.
